// 05 — how i lead
Five rules I don't bend.

      
The standards I hold my team and myself to. They're why the work ships clear, on-brand and on time.

    

    

      
01
Strategy before pixels
No brief, no Figma. Understanding the problem is the work.

      
02
Clear in 3 seconds
If the viewer doesn't know what to do, it goes back.

      
03
Systems over hero pieces
Build once, scale everywhere. Consistency is a feature.

      
04
Words are design
Great copy with great design beats either alone.

      
05
Unblock, don't bottleneck
A good week is one where the team never waited on me.
